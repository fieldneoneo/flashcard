## ✨ New Features

- 📥 **Share to import** — other apps can now hand a CSV or TSV file to the app through the Android share sheet or "Open with". The file opens directly in the import preview, where you pick the category and confirm

## 🔧 Changes

- 📦 This fork now has its own application id (`com.fieldneoneo.flashcard`), so it installs next to the upstream CardPop app instead of replacing it. To bring your cards over, make a backup in CardPop and restore it here
